## Draft 1 — r/devvit feedback thread (or the event Discord)

**Title:** Slog — a daily dungeon where all of Reddit steers one snail (one move each)

Body:
> Built for the Game of the Day hackathon. Every day at 12:00 UTC a new floor opens; everyone in the subreddit sees the same board and the shared hero, and each player gets one move. Moves are attributed in a log, salt costs hearts, dew heals, and the burrow clears the floor for everyone. Training Tunnels give unlimited solo practice.
>
> Try it: https://www.reddit.com/r/slogslogslog_dev/comments/1y1qo2g/slog_floor_1_the_long_way_down/
>
> I'd love blunt feedback on two things: (1) does one-move-per-day feel meaningful or pointless at low population, and (2) does the hive-suggestion row (players who already moved point the next step) make the comment section better or noisier?
>
> Built on Devvit Web: Redis shared state + cron + comment triggers. Happy to trade playtests.

## Draft 2 — r/GamesOnReddit

**Title:** I made a daily game where the whole subreddit controls one snail — one move each

Body:
> Slog is a shared daily dungeon on Reddit: one board, one hero, one move per person per day. Your move is logged by username, the comment section argues the next step, and a new floor appears at noon UTC. There are also unlimited solo Training Tunnels if you just want to play.
>
> Live: https://www.reddit.com/r/slogslogslog_dev/comments/1y1qo2g/slog_floor_1_the_long_way_down/
>
> Feedback welcome — especially on the daily twist rotation (salt storms, dew-rich floors, restless nights).
